# DIT Tracker Launch Announcement

**Subject:**  DIT Tracker is Live – Your New Mobile Command Center

**Body:**

Dear Class Representatives,

We're excited to announce that **DIT Tracker** is now live and ready for use! This mobile-first platform replaces manual spreadsheets with a streamlined system for recording handouts, tracking payments, and managing student groups — all from your phone.

## Get Started in 3 Steps

1. **Install the App:** Open [https://dit-tracker.vercel.app](https://dit-tracker.vercel.app) on your phone → Tap **Share (iOS)** or **Menu ⋮ (Android)** → **"Add to Home Screen"**. It will now open like a native app with no browser bar.
2. **Login:** Use the credentials sent separately by Admin. You'll only see data for your assigned class/term.
3. **Explore:** Your dashboard shows quick actions for submissions/payments and a real-time group payment progress widget.

## Key Features Built for You

- **One-Tap Submissions:** Record handouts in <30 seconds with auto-invoice generation
- **Group Filtering:** Instantly view/pay students by shift, lab, or dorm (e.g., "Morning Shift")
- **Offline Mode:** Search student lists without internet; sync when reconnected
- **PDF Invoices:** Download professional receipts for every transaction
- **Haptic Feedback:** Feel confirmation sounds/vibrations on successful actions
